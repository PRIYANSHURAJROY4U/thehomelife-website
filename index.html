Ideas for your home

Three simple ways to elevate a room

Before you buy anything new, these ideas often make the biggest difference.

  1. Start with light

    Layer a ceiling light with a lamp or two at eye level. Warm, low light makes a room feel calm in the evening.

  2. Layer textures

    Mix soft and natural materials such as cotton, linen, wood and cane, so the room feels inviting and lived in.

  3. Add something living

    A plant or fresh flowers bring colour and life to a corner, and they need very little space.
