About HomeLife

A third-generation home furnishing business in Jayanagar, Bengaluru, making beautiful homes more beautiful for over 10 years.

Our story

Three generations of home furnishing

HomeLife is a home furnishing business in Jayanagar, Bengaluru, built on three generations of experience in the trade. For more than 10 years we have been helping families furnish their homes with customised curtains, wallpapers, blinds, mattresses, sofas and upholstery fabrics.

Our expert team makes every order to suit you, from made-to-order curtains and blinds to new sofas and sofa repairs. We come to your home, help you choose, and deliver to your doorstep, on time, every time.

We also work with interior designers and architects, and we'd love to grow together.
